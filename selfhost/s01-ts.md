## Silent divergences
The suite passed s01, and still did not decide everything. Comparing s01 with duramen on
mutated records (`selfhost/agree.mjs`) found 15 records they read differently, in 9 kinds:

| What differed | duramen 0.2.0 | s01 | 0.3.0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| a file with an unknown version beside one with a known version | P023 and P047 | P023 | P023 only (s01's) |
| lines under a clause the statement does not take (`example` under `op`, `table` under `duramen`) | ignored under `text`, `example` and `table`, P006 under other keywords | P006 for each | ignored, always: one mistake, one diagnostic (D-011) |
| the lines under an example whose first line is malformed | not read | read | read (s01's) |
| a title that does not end with a quote | P005 | P004 | P005 (duramen's) |
| `input y from "unclosed` | P049 | P004 | P049 (duramen's) |
| indented lines after `input <path> from "<file>"` | P006 | taken as text | P006 (duramen's) |
| an `example` or `table` under an open item with malformed content | T003 only | P009, P011, P013 | T003 only (duramen's; the spec said "whatever it holds") |
| a second `text` clause in one statement | silently dropped | read too | P052 (neither) |
| a table row of only `\|` | a row with one empty cell | P014 | a separator (neither) |

Each is pinned in 0.3.0 with an example. Two of the nine went s01's way, four duramen's, and
three neither's; in five of them duramen itself changed.

## What this run taught
A first blind build of a 1,400-line checker passed its whole suite in ten minutes and
reproduced the suite that judged it. The suite was not the limit of what it was asked: its 19
choices and the 15 records it read differently from duramen were the useful output, and most
of them were places where the specification, or duramen itself, had never decided. The one
defect in the brief was duramen's own rendering, which only a builder reading the examples
closely could find. `duramen diff` then called 0.2.0 to 0.3.0 "tightening" until it learned
to run the old examples through the new oracle: two examples had been replaced rather than
edited, so no text showed that their behavior had changed.
