## The four failures, triaged against 0.12.0's words

All four expect a problem in a table's header row to be reported at the `table` line; every
program reports it at the header row's line. REQ-SY-012's words do not say which line, but its
examples do, and the examples are part of the brief:

| case | expected | answered | decided by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `syntax-table-duplicate-header` (`\| x \| x \|`) | P013 at the `table` line | P013 at the header row | REQ-SY-012 example 3: `\| x \| x \|` at line 20 gets `s.duramen:20: error P013` |
| `syntax-table-invalid-header-stops` (`\| bad.path \| result.x ± -1 \|`) | P013 at the `table` line | P013 at the header row | example 3: `\| a.b \| result \|` at line 11 gets P013 at line 11 |
| `syntax-table-prior-tolerance-persists` (`\| x ± 1 \| invalid.path \|`) | P010 and P013 at the `table` line | both at the header row | example 5: `\| a ± 1 \| b! \| result ± x \|` at line 9 gets P010 and P013 at line 9 |
| `syntax-table-header-tolerance-rest-of-cell` (`\| result.x ± 1 extra \|`) | P010 at the `table` line | P010 at the header row | example 3: `\| x \| result ± 1 2 \| …` at line 17 gets P010 at line 17 |

Each is **the outside suite is wrong**: the brief decides the line, by example. (s23 recorded
this very point as its choice C-20, "the line is only visible in examples, not stated in the
words", and s26, round twelve's B, as its C-17.)

## What the suite left out

The writer's notes ([`../loop/r6/NOTES.md`](../loop/r6/NOTES.md)) list what it did not assert,
by open item (OPEN-RQ-001 to 004, OPEN-RC-001 and 002, OPEN-SU-001, OPEN-JU-001 and 002, and
request lines of other white space) and seven corners it found the words did not decide. One of
those seven is a reading that split the builds: a `from` name that leaves the record's folder and
comes back (round eleven's reading 4). Two more, members named twice in JSON and members a
request does not name, were probed in rounds eleven to thirteen and split no one. The suite did
not reach the other readings rounds eleven to thirteen found (a `from` line with no white
space after `from`, a quoted name that is not a JSON string, a `..` in the top folder, a name
ending in `/`, an empty first word of an oracle command, where the oracle's files are and what
its standard input is), so the agreement above says nothing about them.
